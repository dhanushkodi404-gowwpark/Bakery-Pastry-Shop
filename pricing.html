Plan FAQs

Subscription Questions

Can I pause my subscription?
Yes — pause or skip any week from your account dashboard. Changes must be made by Wednesday 6 PM for that Friday's delivery.
Do you deliver to my area?
We currently deliver within 15 miles of our bakery. Enter your postcode at checkout to confirm service. Outside our zone? Choose pickup from the bakery instead.
What if I'm not home during delivery?
Deliveries arrive in an insulated bag on your porch between 6:00–9:00 AM. Your bread stays fresh for hours. We'll text you a photo confirmation on drop-off.
Can I switch plans?
Absolutely. Upgrade, downgrade, or switch plans anytime — changes take effect on your next billing cycle.
