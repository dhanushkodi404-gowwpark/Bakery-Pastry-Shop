Four Ingredients Only Flour · Water · Salt · Time
The Craft

Real Bread Has Nothing to Hide.

We believe true bread requires only four honest ingredients — and the discipline to let time do its work.

Every loaf rests through a 36-hour cold fermentation that develops deep caramelized crust notes, an open honeycombed crumb, and effortless digestion. No commercial yeast, no dough conditioners, no shortcuts.

  • 01
    Stoneground Organic Flour Milled gently between volcanic stones to preserve the wheat germ and natural enzymes.
  • 02
    Filtered Well Water Triple-filtered, balanced minerals, perfect for vigorous wild yeast colonies.
  • 03
    Guérande Grey Salt Hand-harvested sea crystals from Brittany, France with natural trace minerals.
  • 04
    10-Year Mother Levain A living symbiosis of wild yeasts and lactic acid bacteria, nurtured daily.
