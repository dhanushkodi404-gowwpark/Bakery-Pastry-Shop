Dawn to Dusk Narrative

A Day in the Hearth: Four Defining Rituals

Before the neighborhood wakes, our bakers follow ancient rhythms of fermentation, steam, and stone. Follow the transformation of ancient grains into living food.

04:30 AM
Ritual #01 · Awakening

Stone Milling & Levain Feeding

Heirloom Einkorn and Khorasan wheat are freshly stone-ground on site. Our 100-year-old sourdough starter is fed pure water and aerated for morning fermentation.

06:15 AM
Ritual #02 · Lamination

27-Fold French Butter Sheeting

Normandy dry butter is sheeted at precise 16°C between fermented dough layers. Three geometric letter turns create the crisp micro-layers of our signature viennoiserie.

07:00 AM
Ritual #03 · Hearth Pull

The Dawn Stone Hearth Release

Scored with razor blades, the proofed boules enter our 480°F basalt deck ovens. High-pressure steam envelops the crust, yielding dark blistered ears and singing crackles.

10:30 AM
Ritual #04 · Patisserie

Botanical Cake Sculpting

Our pastry chefs hand-pipe Tahitian diplomat creams, temper Valrhona dark chocolates, and adorn celebratory cakes with organic flowers sourced from regional farms.

Atelier Etiquette

Visiting & Photographing Our Baking Studio

We believe artisanal baking is living performance art. Here is helpful guidance for photographing your experience at FlourHouse.

Casual & Guest Photography

Smartphone photography and personal videos are warmly encouraged throughout our front parlor, bread counters, and courtyard tables. Feel free to capture the bread pulling rituals from the viewing windows.

Golden Morning Light Hours

The natural northern skylight in our salon is most vibrant between 08:00 AM and 10:30 AM. If you love photographing pastry textures and coffee steam, this is the most picturesque window.

Commercial & Editorial Shoots

We host cookbook photography, wedding cake tastings, and editorial press shoots on Mondays when our main hearth is resting. Please contact our atelier desk in advance to reserve private studio hours.
