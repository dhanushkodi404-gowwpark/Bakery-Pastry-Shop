Common Questions

Custom Cake FAQs

Everything you need to know before placing a custom order.

How far in advance should I order?
For custom celebration cakes we recommend 2–3 weeks notice. For wedding cakes and large tiered orders, please book 6–8 weeks in advance. We do occasionally accommodate rush orders — just ask.
Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?
Yes. We offer gluten-free, vegan, nut-free, and refined-sugar-free options. Please note that our kitchen handles wheat, nuts, and dairy, so we cannot guarantee zero cross-contamination.
What's the deposit and cancellation policy?
A 50% non-refundable deposit secures your date. The balance is due 7 days before pickup. Cancellations made 14+ days before the event can be rescheduled once at no extra charge.
Do you offer delivery?
Yes. Free pickup from our bakery, or white-glove delivery within 15 miles ($35–$75 depending on distance). Tiered cakes and wedding cakes require delivery by our team.
Can I taste the cake before ordering?
Absolutely. We offer tasting boxes ($25) with 6 flavors for wedding cake bookings. The cost is credited toward your final order if you book with us.
