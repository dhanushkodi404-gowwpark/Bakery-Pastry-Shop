Our Story · Since 2014

Honest Bread, Honest Hands,
Honest Ingredients.

FlourHouse began as a single stone-deck oven in a converted garage. A decade later, we're still baking the same way — with wild fermentation, organic grains, and the kind of patience that only comes from loving what you do.

The Beginning

From a Garage Oven to a Neighborhood Institution

In 2014, our founder Martin left a corporate pastry career to bake bread the way his grandmother did — slowly, honestly, and with flour on his sleeves.

The first FlourHouse loaves were sold from a folding table at the Saturday farmers' market. Word spread quickly. Within six months, we'd outgrown the garage and moved into our current home on Hearthstone Avenue — a former 1920s print shop with exposed brick and the perfect corner for a stone-deck oven.

Today, we bake over 800 loaves and 2,000 pastries every week. But the philosophy hasn't changed: four ingredients, thirty-six hours, and zero shortcuts. Every loaf is still scored by hand, every croissant still folded twenty-seven times.

Our Philosophy

Three Promises We Never Break

The principles behind every loaf, pastry, and cake that leaves our kitchen.

Real Ingredients Only

Stoneground organic flour, filtered water, Guérande sea salt, and wild levain. No commercial yeast, no dough conditioners, no preservatives — ever.

Time Over Speed

Our sourdough ferments for 36 cold hours. Our croissants rest overnight. We refuse to rush fermentation — because time is the most important ingredient we use.

Craft Over Scale

Every loaf is scored by hand. Every pastry is folded by a baker, not a machine. We could produce more — but we'd rather produce better.

The Craft

How a FlourHouse Loaf Is Born

Four deliberate steps — each one impossible to rush.

01
Day 1 · 2:00 PM

Starter Feed & Autolyse

Our 10-year-old levain is refreshed with stoneground flour and filtered water. Separately, tomorrow's dough begins a gentle two-hour autolyse — allowing flour and water to bond before any mixing.

02
Day 1 · 6:00 PM

Mix, Fold & Bulk Ferment

Dough is mixed gently, then folded every 45 minutes to build strength without oxidizing the crumb. It rests overnight in our cold room at 4°C for a slow, flavor-building bulk ferment.

03
Day 2 · 3:30 AM

Shape, Proof & Score

Boules are shaped by hand, placed in wicker bannetons, and given a final cold proof. At dawn, each loaf is turned out, dusted with rice flour, and slashed with a razor lame in our signature pattern.

04
Day 2 · 4:30 AM

Stone Hearth Bake

Loaves are loaded onto 450°F basalt stone decks with pressurized steam. Twenty minutes later, they emerge blistered, golden, and singing — ready for the morning counter.
