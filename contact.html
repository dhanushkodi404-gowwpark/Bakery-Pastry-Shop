Quick Answers

Common Questions

Do I need a reservation to visit?
No reservation needed — walk-ins are always welcome. Our busiest hours are Saturday mornings, so if you want to avoid the queue, visit on weekdays before 10 AM.
Can I request a special order?
Absolutely. Use the form above to describe what you're looking for, or email us at hello@flourhouse.com. Custom cakes require 2–3 weeks advance notice.
Do you sell wholesale to restaurants?
Yes! We supply fresh bread and pastries to several local cafés and restaurants. Email us with your weekly volume and delivery address for a wholesale quote.
Is there parking nearby?
Yes — free 2-hour street parking is available on Hearthstone Avenue, plus a public lot one block away on Baker Street. We also have bike racks out front.
